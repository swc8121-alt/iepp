# TRP 2.0 Design Principles

1. **Continuity over resemblance.** Similar outputs do not establish canonical lineage.
2. **Canonicality over originality language.** The protocol verifies a policy-selected lineage, not metaphysical originality.
3. **Capabilities before conclusions.** Every result names what the attacker knows and controls.
4. **Freshness is composite.** Challenges, predecessor state, counters, runtime evidence and entropy have different roles; none should be treated as a magic identity source.
5. **One winner is a registry property.** Stochastic divergence cannot replace atomic canonical selection.
6. **Compromise boundaries are results, not embarrassments.** A5 success at L1 is useful because it states exactly where stronger mechanisms are needed.
7. **Empiricism is bounded.** Experiments can falsify claims and build evidence but do not create a hardness theorem by repetition.
8. **Evidence levels prevent claim inflation.** L1 results do not automatically imply L2, L3 or L4 security.
